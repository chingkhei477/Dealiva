Transparency

How Dealiva earns, in plain words

Retailers pay a commission to websites that send them customers. When you shop through a Dealiva link, the retailer pays that commission to Dealiva through its affiliate programme.

We keep part of it to run Dealiva and pass the rest to you as cashback. That's the whole model. There are no fees, subscriptions or paid upgrades.

What cashback is, and what it isn't

  • A partial return on purchases you were already planning to make.
  • Paid only after the retailer confirms your order.
  • Not income, an investment or a way to make money.
  • Not guaranteed. Retailers can decline orders that are returned, cancelled or don't meet the offer conditions.
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Dealiva free to use?

Yes. There's no sign-up fee, subscription or charge of any kind. Dealiva is paid by retailers, not by you.

Why do I need a Dealiva Click ID?

Your Click ID links your visit to the retailer with your order. We use it to match the retailer's report to your cashback claim.

How long does cashback take?

Retailers usually confirm an order after its return or cancellation window closes, typically 30 to 90 days after purchase. Each offer shows its expected confirmation time. Once an order is confirmed, we pay your cashback within 7 working days.

Why could my cashback be declined?

Common reasons include a cancelled or returned order, using a coupon the offer doesn't allow, a purchase that wasn't tracked (for example, because another cashback or coupon link was clicked after Dealiva), or an order that didn't meet the offer conditions.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You register each purchase using our claim form with your email address and UPI ID. We use these details only to process your cashback and keep you updated.
