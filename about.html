About Dealiva

A simpler, more honest way to get cashback

Dealiva is an Indian cashback platform. We partner with retailers through their affiliate programmes and share our commission with shoppers who buy through our links.

What we do

Online shopping already involves enough small print. Dealiva keeps cashback simple: we list offers we've verified, explain every condition in plain language, and pay confirmed cashback straight to your UPI ID.

We don't sell products, hold your money in a wallet or charge fees. Retailers handle your order, delivery, returns and refunds. We handle the cashback.

How we're funded

When you buy through a Dealiva link, the retailer pays us a commission. We keep part of it to run the service and pass the rest to you as cashback. That's our only source of income from shoppers. It never costs you anything.

Our principles

What we stand for

Transparency

Every rate, condition and status is explained upfront, in plain language, before you shop.

Fairness

No fees and no minimum payout. When the retailer confirms your order, we pay your cashback.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to pay you, and we never sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
