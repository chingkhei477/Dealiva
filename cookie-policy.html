This Cookie Policy explains how Dealiva uses cookies and similar technologies, such as browser local storage, on dealiva.in, and how retailers' tracking works when you follow an offer link.

1. What these technologies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage works in a similar way but stays on your device and isn't sent to a server automatically. Both help websites remember information between pages and visits.

2. What Dealiva uses

NameTypePurposeDuration
dealiva_clicksLocal storage (essential)Remembers your most recent Dealiva Click IDs and retailer names in your browser, so you can fill them into the claim form. It isn't sent to us unless you submit it in a form.Until you clear your browser data. Up to 20 entries are kept.
dealiva_consentLocal storage (essential)Remembers whether you allowed or declined optional advertising measurement, when it's offered.Until you clear your browser data

Dealiva doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies unless you allow them. We don't use cookies to identify you personally.

3. Optional advertising measurement

We may use the Meta Pixel to understand whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead people to Dealiva. If we do, a banner will ask for your permission and the Meta Pixel will load only if you choose Allow. If you decline, it won't load. Meta's use of this data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Retailer and affiliate network cookies

When you follow an offer link, the retailer or its affiliate network sets cookies on its own website to record that you arrived from Dealiva. This is how your purchase is tracked for cashback. These cookies are controlled by the retailer or network, not by Dealiva, and are covered by their own cookie and privacy policies. If you block them, your purchase may not be tracked.

5. Managing cookies and storage

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing Dealiva's storage removes the saved Click IDs from your browser. Please note your Click ID first if you still need to register a purchase. Blocking cookies on retailer websites can stop cashback from being tracked.

6. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy if we change the technologies we use. Questions? Email support@dealiva.in.
